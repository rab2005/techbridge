About TechBridge

Connecting university inventions with the people who can build businesses around them.

The problem

Universities invent countless new technologies, and most of them never get used. Usually it's not because the ideas are bad. Nobody outside the lab knows they exist, or nobody can see the commercial application. Researchers are experts at inventing, not at starting companies, and the entrepreneurs who could build something from these inventions rarely find them. When they do, the listings are written in dense scientific and legal language that hides the business opportunity.

What TechBridge does

TechBridge brings technologies that universities are offering for licensing into one searchable place, and summarizes each one in plain English: what it is, what you could sell, who would buy it, and how hard it might be to bring to market. Search by keyword, or describe a problem you want to solve and let TechBridge find technologies that match.

How it works

  • Listings come straight from each university's technology transfer office.
  • TechBridge checks for new listings every day, and marks technologies a university has removed as "no longer listed".
  • Plain-English summaries are written by Claude, an AI model from Anthropic. They're estimates, so always confirm details with the university's licensing office.
  • "Describe your problem" uses an open-source AI model that matches by meaning, not just keywords.
